NIT Solutions HR Policy

Annual Leave:
Employees receive 15 days of annual leave every year.
Annual leave must be requested through the employee portal.
Employees should request annual leave at least 7 days in advance.

Sick Leave:
Employees receive 8 sick leaves every year.
A medical certificate may be required for extended sick leave.
Employees must notify their manager when taking sick leave.

Notice Period:
The notice period for permanent employees is 60 days.
Employees must submit resignation through the HR portal.
The notice period begins from the date resignation is formally accepted.

Work From Home:
Employees can work from home up to 3 days per week.
Manager approval is required.
Employees must remain available during normal working hours.

Office Timing:
Normal office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
Employees are expected to complete their required working hours.

Medical Insurance:
Health insurance is provided to employees and their dependents.
Employees can add eligible dependents through the HR portal.
